## Design Context

### Users
TwoBee is an Italian growth consultancy for SMEs. Its Merch section specifically addresses marketers, designers, creatives and people working in agencies, as confirmed by the project owner. Visitors should recognise the jokes, explore the upcoming categories and eventually browse a small shop.

### Brand Personality
Playful, bold, ironic. For Merch, humour comes from briefs, calls and everyday creative work, not generic jokes or childish visual language.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the existing TwoBee identity: brand yellow #FFC501, near-black #0B0B0C, off-white #FBFAF9, League Spartan display and Inter body. Merch has a deliberately bold, animated hero and an expressive graphic language. Keep shared navigation and footer consistent with the existing site. Support Italian and English, small screens, keyboard access and reduced motion.

### Design Principles
1. Make the hero unmistakable: short, oversized type and a choreographed entrance.
2. Use creative-industry humour that feels specific to the audience.
3. Do not invent products, prices, availability or checkout functionality. The owner will provide the catalogue progressively.
4. Treat illustrations as editorial artwork, not photographs or representations of available products.
5. Keep motion purposeful, controllable and respectful of reduced-motion settings.
